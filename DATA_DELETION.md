# Request Account & Data Deletion — Bible Trivia

If you signed in to Bible Trivia with Google or Apple, you can request permanent deletion of your account and all associated cloud data.

## What gets deleted
- Your account (Google/Apple sign-in link)
- Synced progress, coin balance, and purchase/restore history
- Your leaderboard entry and display name

Local data stored only on your device (offline quiz stats, settings) is not affected by this request; uninstall the App to remove that data as well.

## How to request deletion

### Option 1: In the App (fastest)
Go to **Settings > Account & Sync > Delete Account & Data** while signed in. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, cloud progress, purchase history, and leaderboard entries.

### Option 2: Submit a request online
[**Click here to request data deletion**](https://github.com/Allwell987/BibleTrivia/issues/new?title=Data%20Deletion%20Request&body=Please%20delete%20my%20Bible%20Trivia%20account%20and%20data.%0A%0AAccount%20email%20used%20to%20sign%20in%3A%20%0APlatform%20(iOS%2FAndroid)%3A%20)

### Option 3: Email us
Email [support@bibletrivia.app](mailto:support@bibletrivia.app) with the subject "Delete My Data" from the email address associated with your account.

## Processing time
Requests are processed within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation once your account and data have been deleted.
