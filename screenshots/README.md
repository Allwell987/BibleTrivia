# Screenshots

This folder is for app store screenshots. To capture screenshots:

## iOS Screenshots
1. Open the app on an iOS Simulator or device
2. Press Cmd+S (Mac) to save a screenshot
3. Or use the built-in screenshot tool

## Android Screenshots
1. Open the app on an Android device
2. Press Power + Volume Down buttons simultaneously
3. Screenshots will be saved to your gallery

## Required Sizes

### iOS App Store
- iPhone 6.5" Display: 1284 x 2778 px
- iPhone 5.5" Display: 1242 x 2208 px
- iPad Pro 12.9" (2nd gen): 2048 x 2732 px
- iPad Pro 11" (1st gen): 1668 x 2388 px

### Google Play Store
- Phone screenshot: 1080 x 1920 px
- 7" tablet screenshot: 1200 x 1920 px
- 10" tablet screenshot: 2560 x 1800 px

## Recommended Screens to Capture
1. Home screen with daily verse
2. Quiz in progress
3. Result screen with high score
4. Leaderboard
5. Statistics/Achievements
6. Settings screen
